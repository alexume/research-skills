# Project Argument Profile

Use this artifact to record project-specific intellectual choices without embedding them in reusable skills. Create it before full discussion drafting when the contribution, debate, or architecture remains open.

## Core Contract

Produce `project/project_argument_profile.qmd` with:

```markdown
## Research Question

## Central Empirical Or Historical Tension

## Selected Contribution

## Alternative Contributions Considered

## Debate To Transform

## Central Relations And Mediations

| Processes or results connected | Relation | Actors or mediations | Period and scale | Evidence | Missing support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Claims Permitted, Contested, And Excluded

## Required Sources And Evidence

## Preferred And Prohibited Formulations

## Editorial Family And Secondary Audience

## Author Decisions

## Approval Status
```

Set approval status to `provisional`, `author-approved`, or `superseded`. Record the date and the decision source. Do not infer author approval from silence or from the existence of a draft.

## Alternative Architecture Comparison

When the central contribution is unresolved, propose two to four genuinely different architectures before recommending prose. For each state:

- central thesis;
- intellectual problem and debate payoff;
- sequence of argumentative moves;
- relations and mediations required;
- evidence already available;
- exact sources or author decisions still needed;
- claims or sections to demote, remove, or preserve;
- editorial family and secondary audience;
- advantages, costs, and risk of contribution loss.

End with a comparison matrix and a reasoned recommendation. Do not select among defensible contributions on the author's behalf.

## Contribution-Preservation Test

Before narrowing, parking, or deleting a central bridge, ask:

1. Does the paper retain a specific contribution to a debate without this bridge?
2. Does the remaining argument explain the evidence or merely audit an indicator, method, or source?
3. Does the change remove the historical, theoretical, distributional, or socioecological payoff promised by the introduction?
4. Could a specific retrievable source or author decision preserve a defensible version?

If narrowing would collapse a central contribution, mark the architecture `provisional` or `blocked`, retrieve available evidence, and issue an exact source or author request. Do not pass the interpretation gate by silently choosing the least ambitious argument.

## Integration Test

When an architecture claims interdisciplinary or relational integration, verify that the domains are connected through a named mediation and supported sequence. Adjacent paragraphs about economic, social, institutional, and biophysical processes do not establish integration by themselves.

Do not require every project to integrate every domain. Apply the test only to the connections the paper claims as part of its contribution.
