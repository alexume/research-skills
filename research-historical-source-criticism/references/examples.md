# Worked Source-Criticism Examples

## 1. Nineteenth-century municipal census

### Intended use

Estimate population and occupational structure by municipality in 1870.

### Critical reconstruction

- The provincial government commissioned the census for taxation and conscription.
- Enumerators recorded legal residence rather than people present on census day.
- Military personnel, mobile laborers, and some Indigenous settlements were tabulated separately or omitted.
- Occupations were recorded only for household heads in several municipalities.
- Two municipalities changed boundaries between the census and the outcome data.
- The digitized table is a modern transcription without links to page images for corrected cells.

### Evidentiary decision

The source can support bounded municipal population totals after boundary harmonization and uncertainty flags. It cannot support individual-level occupational participation or direct comparison of occupational shares where enumeration rules differ. Tax purpose and omission patterns should become part of the interpretation rather than a generic footnote.

## 2. Forestry ministry extraction report

### Intended use

Measure regional timber extraction and infer deforestation pressure between 1920 and 1940.

### Critical reconstruction

- Reported volumes cover licensed extraction from public forests.
- Private land, household fuelwood, illegal cutting, and some concessions are excluded.
- Regional offices copied concession declarations with irregular inspections.
- Units shift from local cubic measures to metric tons in 1931.
- Administrative capacity expanded after 1928, raising recorded extraction independently of physical extraction.

### Evidentiary decision

The reports directly measure administratively registered timber flows, not total extraction or forest loss. They can support analysis of state-visible commercialization and regulatory capacity. Claims about total biophysical pressure require conversion assumptions, private and informal extraction evidence, forest-cover sources, and sensitivity bounds.
