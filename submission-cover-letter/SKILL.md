# Skill: Submission Cover Letter

You prepare cover letters for academic journal submission.

Use this skill after journal fit has been assessed and the manuscript is ready or conditionally ready for submission.

## Core principle

A cover letter should help the editor understand fit, contribution, and readiness. It should not oversell the manuscript or repeat the abstract mechanically.

Use:
- `submission-journal-fit`
- `submission-pre-submission-audit`
- `writing-title-abstract-keywords`
- `shared/journal_profiles.md`

## Workflow

### Step 1: Confirm submission facts
Identify:
- target journal,
- editor name if known,
- manuscript title,
- author names if provided,
- article type,
- word count if available,
- special issue if relevant.

### Step 2: State contribution
Summarize in one paragraph:
- the problem or debate,
- what the paper adds,
- why the evidence or method is distinctive,
- why the contribution fits the journal.

### Step 3: State fit
Connect the manuscript to:
- the journal's audience,
- expected debates,
- methodological standards,
- regional, historical, environmental, development, or political-economic relevance.

### Step 4: Add formal declarations
Include only when appropriate:
- original work statement,
- not under consideration elsewhere,
- ethics approval or not applicable,
- conflict of interest statement,
- data availability statement,
- funding statement,
- suggested reviewers,
- reviewer exclusions.

### Step 5: Keep it concise
Default length: 250-450 words unless journal instructions require otherwise.

## Output format

```markdown
## Submission Facts

## Cover Letter Draft

## Optional Declarations Needed

## Suggested Reviewers or Exclusions

## Risks to Avoid
```

## Rules

- Do not exaggerate novelty.
- Do not claim perfect fit if journal fit is uncertain.
- Do not include unsupported impact claims.
- Do not write a long literature review.
- If the manuscript has known weaknesses, do not disclose them unless ethically or procedurally required; instead, ensure they are fixed before submission.
