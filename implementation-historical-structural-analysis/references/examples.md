# Examples

## Agrarian transformation

A design proposes 1930-1955, 1956-1974, and 1975-1990 periods. Establish land concentration, tenancy, crop composition, labor relations, credit, and infrastructure separately. Check whether census-definition changes explain apparent breaks. Compare frontier and consolidated regions, trace land-policy implementation and actor responses, and evaluate whether market integration or coercive property change better explains regional divergence.

## Socioecological transition

A design proposes that export infrastructure reorganized a watershed economy. Combine material-flow series, land-cover evidence, concession records, transport maps, company reports, and local administrative sources. Distinguish extraction growth from displacement across boundaries, test whether infrastructure preceded changes in land use and labor, and retain contradictions between company production records and fiscal or ecological evidence.
