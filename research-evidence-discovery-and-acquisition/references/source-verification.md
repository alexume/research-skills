# Candidate and Acquisition Verification

## Catalogue-level verification

Confirm creator, title, dates, collection hierarchy, custodian, stable locator, access status, and described coverage. Catalogue metadata can support discovery and planning but does not justify content-level claims.

## Dataset-level verification

Confirm:

- release or version;
- producer and distributor;
- unit of observation and population;
- time and geographic coverage;
- variables and classifications;
- documentation and file relationships;
- license and redistribution status;
- update or revision history.

## File-level verification

After acquisition record:

- provider filename and stable local name;
- byte size and format;
- checksum where feasible;
- compression and expected members;
- encoding, sheets, tables, or database objects;
- whether the file opens and matches its documentation;
- deviations from catalogue description.

Do not modify provider files to make them open. Preserve them and create repaired or converted derivatives downstream.

## Rejection reasons

Use controlled reasons such as:

- wrong period, geography, population, variable, or resolution;
- derivative with untraceable provenance;
- incompatible definitions or boundaries;
- undocumented version;
- insufficient rights or privacy protection;
- corrupted or incomplete file;
- catalogue-only or inaccessible;
- duplicate of a better verified source.
