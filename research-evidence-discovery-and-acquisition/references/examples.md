# Examples

## Missing municipal agricultural statistics

Requirement `REQ-D07` needs municipality-crop-year area and output for 1920-1950. Search national statistical-office catalogues, agricultural censuses, provincial yearbooks, and archival statistical series using historical province and crop names. A digitized national total is `partial`, not `satisfied`, because it cannot support municipal spatial patterns. Record inaccessible census volumes as `requested` and define whether provincial totals permit a narrower claim.

## Missing environmental series

Requirement `REQ-S04` needs watershed-level forest change before satellite coverage. Candidate maps, forestry inventories, cadastral records, and later reconstructed rasters must be registered as different source types. A published reconstruction is not equivalent to original maps: verify its inputs and methods, acquire lawful files and documentation, and route boundary and classification decisions to historical measurement.
