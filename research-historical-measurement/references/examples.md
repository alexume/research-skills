# Worked Historical Measurement Examples

## 1. Municipal land concentration

### Concept

Concentration of recognized agricultural landholdings, not total control over land or elite political power.

### Source problem

Cadastres list taxable titled parcels. Communal, public, informal, and disputed land are incompletely observed. Municipalities split between 1900 and 1920, and parcel owners can hold multiple parcels.

### Measurement decisions

- Preserve parcel area, owner identifier, tenure status, and cadastral year.
- Construct parcel-level and owner-level concentration separately.
- Report cadastral coverage as registered area divided by independently estimated municipal area.
- Harmonize municipalities to a declared reference geography using land-area weights, with unmatched shares.
- Treat the Gini as concentration within registered private land, not inequality in total access to land.
- Test sensitivity to owner linkage, communal-land inclusion, boundary crosswalk, and low-coverage municipalities.

### Permitted claim

The measure supports comparisons of concentration in recorded private holdings where cadastral coverage is adequate. It cannot alone establish elite power, actual land use, or inequality among all rural households.

## 2. Timber extraction and socioecological pressure

### Concept

Commercially recorded timber extraction as one component of regional material throughput.

### Source problem

Reports mix cubic feet, local volume units, metric tons, and monetary values. Species and moisture differ, and licensed extraction omits informal fuelwood and some private forests.

### Measurement decisions

- Keep physical quantities separate from monetary values.
- Convert volumes to mass only with species- and moisture-appropriate density ranges.
- Produce low, central, and high conversion scenarios.
- Separate logs, processed timber, fuelwood, and re-exports to avoid double counting.
- Report registered extraction per land area and per population only as different analytical ratios.
- Do not infer deforestation without land-cover or stock-change evidence.

### Permitted claim

The harmonized series measures the scale and composition of state-visible commercial timber flows. Broader claims about total extraction, forest degradation, or unequal ecological exchange require additional system boundaries and evidence.
