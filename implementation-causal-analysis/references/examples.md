# Causal Implementation Examples

## IV: Transport access and market integration

Use a historically justified instrument only after defining the complier population and direct geographic channels. Report first stage, reduced form, 2SLS, weak-IV-robust inference, and sensitivity to instrument subsets and spatial clustering. Do not interpret distance as exogenous by construction.

## RDD: Eligibility threshold for a conservation program

Verify that the historical threshold governed assignment, inspect support and manipulation, estimate locally with robust bias-corrected inference, and report bandwidth sensitivity. Interpret the effect for units near the threshold, not the entire region.

## Panel: Commodity prices and land-use change

Unit and time fixed effects remove only specified additive confounding. Report within-unit exposure variation, lag choice, switchers, serial and spatial dependence, measurement changes, and alternative first-difference or trend specifications tied to actual threats.

## Synthetic control: A regional extraction ban

Define donors before post-treatment comparison, exclude contaminated regions, demonstrate pre-fit and weight concentration, run credible placebos and leave-one-donor-out checks, and limit the causal claim to the treated region and historical intervention.
