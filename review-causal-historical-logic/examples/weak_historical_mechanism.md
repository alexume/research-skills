# Example: Weak Historical Mechanism

## Problem

The paper argues that commodity price increases caused agrarian transformation and frontier expansion.

However, the manuscript does not clearly explain:
- through which actors,
- institutions,
- or political processes
these transformations occurred.

Commodity booms do not automatically generate:
- land concentration,
- frontier settlement,
- or agrarian restructuring.

These outcomes depend on:
- state policy,
- property-rights regimes,
- infrastructure,
- labour systems,
- credit access,
- and the political power of agrarian elites.

The manuscript treats the commodity shock as if it directly produced territorial transformation without mediation.

## Why this matters

Historical processes are mediated and uneven.

Without identifying:
- actors,
- institutions,
- and mechanisms,
the explanation becomes deterministic.

The paper risks reducing structural agrarian transformations to price movements alone.

## Diagnostic Labels

WEAK MECHANISM

SHOCK DETERMINISM

OMITTED MEDIATION

## Suggested Improvement

The manuscript should:
- specify the actors benefiting from the boom,
- explain how institutions mediated access to land or resources,
- distinguish frontier expansion from intensification,
- and situate commodity dynamics within broader agrarian and political transformations.

The argument would benefit from a clearer connection between:
- commodity shocks,
- institutional structures,
- and territorial outcomes.
