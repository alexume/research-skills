# Review Presets

## critical referee — ecological economics paper
- Skills to activate: `review-reviewer-core`, `review-ecological-economics`, `review-causal-historical-logic`
- Focus: conceptual coherence, indicator interpretation, causal overreach, scale effects, and contribution relative to ecological economics debates.
- Expected output: a critical referee report with diagnostic labels, major revisions, minor revisions, and a final assessment.

## critical referee — historical political economy paper
- Skills to activate: `review-reviewer-core`, `review-causal-historical-logic`, `review-agrarian-environmental-history`
- Focus: mechanism clarity, historical periodization, institutions, agrarian structure, and interpretation of causal claims.
- Expected output: a rigorous referee report emphasizing structural weaknesses and historical plausibility.

## constructive coauthor — early draft
- Skills to activate: `review-reviewer-core`, `research-hypothesis-refinement`, `literature-review`
- Focus: argument structure, hypothesis clarity, literature positioning, and practical revision sequencing.
- Expected output: a constructive memo with suggested restructuring steps, missing evidence, and revision priorities.

## constructive supervisor — doctoral proposal
- Skills to activate: `review-doctoral-proposal`, `review-reviewer-core`, `research-strategy-choice`, and relevant disciplinary lenses.
- Focus: question, contribution, literature, design, sources/data, feasibility, risks, chapter plan, and next steps.
- Expected output: a supportive but rigorous supervisory memo with comments that can be shared with the student.

## editor mode — journal fit
- Skills to activate: `review-reviewer-core`, `literature-review`, `review-ecological-economics` or `review-agrarian-environmental-history` as needed
- Focus: journal fit, novelty, likely reviewer objections, and revision feasibility.
- Expected output: a brief editor-style assessment with fit, strengths, liabilities, and decision recommendation.

## editor mode — internal screening
- Skills to activate: `review-reviewer-core` and a relevant disciplinary lens when needed
- Focus: minimum evaluable academic form before external review; question, contribution, method, debate, evidence-claim fit, and structural maturity.
- Expected output: a short internal editorial assessment that reconstructs the manuscript, identifies its strongest asset, diagnoses the main blockers, and recommends either external review, no external review, or deep reformulation before external review.
- Fixed-form rule: when the internal form cannot accept an `X`, highlight only the criteria the manuscript clearly satisfies and keep author comments separate from editor comments.
