Use this guide to select authorial references by function.
Do not load every reference for every task.

## Selection by Task

### Colombian or Latin American agrarian history

Prioritize:

1. `Urrego_2021.pdf`
2. `Urrego_2025.pdf`
3. the most relevant Infante et al. paper for comparative framing

### Trade, social metabolism, or ecological-economic interpretation

Prioritize:

1. `Urrego_2025.pdf`
2. `Infante et al_2022.pdf`
3. `Infante et al_2024.pdf` or `Infante et al_2025.pdf` according to the outcome studied

### Introduction or discussion

Use references that match the manuscript's geography and mechanism.
Study how they move from a concrete pattern to a broader historical interpretation.
Do not reproduce their exact opening structure.

### Results or empirical interpretation

Prioritize references using comparable evidence.
Study how numbers, periods, and turning points are connected to mechanisms.
Avoid importing conceptual vocabulary that the new evidence does not require.

### Conclusion

Study how the references scale outward from the case without exceeding the evidence.
Retain limitations and regional specificity.

### Editorial or evaluative prose

The paper corpus is secondary.
Prefer the dedicated editorial protocol or examples supplied for that task.

## Calibration Rules

- Match language when possible.
- Match genre and section before matching topic.
- Prefer one or two strong references over the full corpus.
- Extract patterns of reasoning, not reusable phrases.
- Preserve the new manuscript's object, evidence, and uncertainty.
- When authorial references conflict with current project needs, follow the evidence and argument of the current manuscript.
